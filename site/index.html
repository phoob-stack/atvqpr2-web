ATV Quad Power Racing 2 on PC and in the browser

An unofficial static recompilation of the 2003 Xbox game. It runs from your own copy of the game, at 60 fps.

You need

  • The files from your own USA retail disc. The game is not included.
  • Edge or Chrome with WebGPU. A gamepad is optional.
  • The builder, run once on your PC. See Setup.
